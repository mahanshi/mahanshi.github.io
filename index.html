About
Mahan

Mahan Shishehchi is a composer and violinist based in Tehran. His work explores contemporary composition, orchestral and chamber writing, electroacoustic music, and the meeting point of musical thought and technology.

He is currently pursuing an M.A. in Music Composition at the University of Tehran.
